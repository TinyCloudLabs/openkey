The sign screens (`/widget/sign` and `/widget/embed/sign`) show what an app says about a plain `signMessage()` request: its name, who the request is for, and its reason. The words sit under the verified "Requested by" origin and are marked as unchecked, in these lines: `It calls itself "{name}". OpenKey hasn't checked that name.`, `It says it's asking for "{onBehalfOf}".`, `Its reason: "{reason}"`, and one closing line `OpenKey hasn't checked these.` when two or three are shown (`OpenKey hasn't checked this.` for an on-behalf-of or reason alone). An on-behalf-of equal to the delegatee in the signed message reads `It is asking for "{onBehalfOf}".` Texts are plain text, normalised, stripped of control, bidi and zero-width characters, and cut to 64, 64 and 280 characters. A name that reads as another host is dropped. A name or on-behalf-of that contains a reserved name (TinyCloud, TinyCloud One, OpenKey, TinyChat, Share, Secret Manager), however it is spelled, is dropped unless the requester is a trusted first-party origin. An icon shows only as a PNG, JPEG or WebP data URI of at most 32768 characters, beside a name that is shown. The signed bytes, the Approve tap and the requests to the API are unchanged, and nothing of the presentation is sent to the signer or the API. The widget's `openkey:ready` message carries `presentation: 1`.

Details: TC-3706
