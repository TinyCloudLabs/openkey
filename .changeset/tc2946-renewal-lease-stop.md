Owners can stop renewal leases, and signing out or deleting the account stops them (TC-2946).

- Each lease is recorded before its node is asked to admit it, under the owner's user-row lock, and the approval that admitted it confirms the record (`renewal_lease.admittedAt`). A lease already stopped is never admitted again, and a leased approval whose record is missing or stopped approves nothing. New migration `20261113_0003_renewal_lease_reservation` adds `grantSiwe` (the approved grant's SIWE message text, no signature), `admittedAt`, `neverAdmittedAt` and `leaseAuthorization` (the signed lease, encrypted with the auth secret, kept only until the lease is confirmed or settled, never returned or logged). The table holds the owner's address and the grant's resources for their own Settings page.
- Stopping a lease revokes exactly that lease at its node: an owner revocation (SIWE, URI `ucan:<lease CID>`, valid 5 minutes) signed by the key that signed the lease, sent to `<nodeOrigin>/revoke`. The node stops renewing and drops the time renewal added; the grant is not revoked and ends at its own signed end, or at once if that has passed. A lease whose admission was never confirmed is first admitted again with its stored signed bytes and revoked straight after, under the same lock. A lease counts as stopped only when its node settles it: the revocation succeeded (`revokedAt`), or the node refused both the admission and the revocation (`neverAdmittedAt`). Anything else, including a stored lease that no longer decrypts, leaves it on, and the next attempt tries again.
- Settings shows "Renewing permissions" once the account has a lease: the request's reason, the signed grant's permissions in the approval screen's own sentences, when renewal ends and when it was approved, and "Stop renewal". `GET /api/account/renewal-leases` lists the account's leases that are neither settled nor past their end. `POST /api/account/renewal-leases/:leaseCid/stop` stops one (404 for another account's, an unknown, a stopped or an ended lease; 503 when it isn't settled). A lease an external wallet signed answers 409 `wallet_required` with the revocation message; the same call with `{ siwe, signature }` sends the wallet's signature, which must be on that exact message for that lease and recover to its owner.
- Signing out stops leases before the sessions go: `/sign-out` stops the leases approved in that session, `/revoke-session` those of the session it names, `/revoke-other-sessions` every lease not approved in the current session, `/revoke-sessions` all of them. Sign-out always goes ahead; when it touched any lease its answer gains `renewalLeases: { stopped, notStopped, walletRequired }`, and the dashboard says when a permission is still renewing instead of leaving.
- Account deletion first stops every live lease. Unless every one is settled it deletes nothing and answers 409 `renewal_leases_not_stopped` with `walletRequired` and `nodeUnavailable` counts; deleting again retries only the leases still on. A lease recorded meanwhile is caught under the user lock and refused the same way, so no lease record is removed with the user while its lease can still renew.
