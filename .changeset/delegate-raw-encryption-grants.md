`/delegate` signs a CLI's raw encryption decrypt grant (`service` `tinycloud.encryption` or `encryption`, `path` `urn:tinycloud:encryption:<ownerDid>:<name>`, `space` `encryption` or absent) as a top-level ReCap resource instead of nesting it under the session space, so the delegated session can decrypt. The grant is limited to `tinycloud.encryption/decrypt` on a network the signer owns (`did:pkh:eip155:<chainId>:<address>`) whose name matches `^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9-]*$`; anything else is refused with `invalid_permissions` before signing. The relayed `permissions` report it with `space: "encryption"`, and an owner who unchecks it gets a delegation without it. `/api/delegate/complete` and the managed `/api/delegate` approval now refuse forwarded `permissions` that grant different services, paths, raw networks, or actions than the request prepared at `/api/delegate/prepare` (`baseline-digest-mismatch`); the order of actions and how they are split across entries does not matter, and the session space is bound by the prepared SIWE rather than by this check. `/complete` answers a non-string `prepared.siwe` with 400. On the consent page, unchecking a grant maps correctly for raw networks; Approve stays disabled while the prepared authorization differs from the visible selection (for example after a failed narrowing), and a wallet approval signs the authorization prepared when it started; a request carrying a raw network preselects and checks the requested wallet; a request whose spaces and networks name different accounts (address or chain) is refused before preparing; and a permissions parameter with a malformed entry shows the unreadable-request error. Device authorization still rejects encryption grants.
