The approval for a grant that unlocks and creates one exact encryption key names the key. Its heading reads "Create and use one encryption key: {name}" and its sentence reads "{site} could create the encryption key named {name} and unlock the items encrypted with it, for as long as this approval lasts. This permission covers only that key." A grant on a folder keeps its folder wording.
