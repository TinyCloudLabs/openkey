`/api/delegate/authorize-sign-preview` and `/api/delegate/authorize-sign` check the exact bytes they are about to return or sign against the request the app made at `/authorize-sign-prepare` and the owner's selection, and refuse with `400 signed_exceeds_request` and a `reason` naming the first difference. Both messages must parse under a strict EIP-4361 and EIP-5573 reader that re-serializes them byte for byte (repeated, unknown or out-of-order fields, repeated resources or JSON keys, a second ReCap, padded or non-canonical base64url are refused). The signed message must keep the requested domain and scheme, address, URI, version, chain id, nonce, issued-at, not-before and request id; keep every non-ReCap resource; keep the requested statement with only its ReCap sentence rewritten for the signed capabilities; carry only requested (resource, ability) pairs with their requested caveats, only requested proofs, and only capabilities the owner left selected. The expiration must equal the requested one unless the owner picked another at preview; a later picked time still needs the app's `expiryChangeable: true` and is never available to a CLI handoff. `/authorize-sign-prepare` refuses a request the strict reader cannot read (`reason: "request_unparsable"`) before issuing a context.

Device approvals (`/api/delegate/prepare`, `POST /api/delegate` and `/api/delegate/complete` with `deviceTransactionId`) apply the same check before the bytes reach a wallet, before managed signing, and before completion: the reference is the message the server prepares for the device's stored request, with the requester and chain the device routes write (`cli.tinycloud.xyz`, chain 1), the device's session key, and the signed message's signer, nonce and times; every capability in it is selectable. A page that sends permissions broader than the device asked for (an added action, path, space or grant) is refused with `400 signed_exceeds_request`, and so is a wallet-signed `/complete` message that names another domain or chain; before, only the session key, Node origin and lifetime were compared.

What can now be refused that was accepted before. At `/authorize-sign-prepare`, a request the WASM parser accepted but EIP-4361/EIP-5573 do not allow: a nonce that is not alphanumeric, a chain id with a leading zero, a lower-case `t` or `z` in a timestamp, a request id with characters outside RFC 3986 `pchar`, or a ReCap payload with members other than `att` and `prf`. At preview, narrowing a request whose statement carries text before the ReCap sentence (the regenerated message dropped that text), or that lists another resource before its ReCap (the regenerated message moved the ReCap first). Requests produced by `prepareSession` are unaffected. Details: TC-2198
